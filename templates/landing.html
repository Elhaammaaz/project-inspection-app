Key Features

Building Information

Assessment Items

Detailed inspection items across 21 systems with ratings, weights, risk scores, and automatic percentage calculations.

Compliance Tracking

Track government compliance requirements with Yes/No/Partial status, evidence references, and document uploads.

System Scoring

Automated weighted scoring per system with visual progress indicators and overall building condition assessment.

Test Register

Record all tests performed with acceptance criteria, results, test dates, and next scheduled test tracking.

CAPA Management

Corrective and Preventive Actions with priority levels, due dates, responsibility assignment, and verification tracking.
